NextFour Pickleball — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026
App: NextFour Pickleball (developer: Ashwitha Reddy)

The short version

NextFour does not collect any personal data. The app has no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

To run your sessions, the app stores the following only on your device (in the app's local storage):

  • Player names, skill levels, and team / club / league tags you enter
  • Session details: check-ins, courts, games, scores, and player history
  • If you create an in-app account: the name, email, and password you choose, kept on the device for sign-in

This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, because the app has no backend to send it to.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, sell, rent, or share any data about you or your players. We cannot see your sessions, your players, or how you use the app.

Third parties, ads, and tracking

Version 1.0 of NextFour contains no third-party analytics, no advertising, no tracking technology, and no in-app purchases. No data is shared with third parties because no data leaves the device.

Deleting your data

Your data is yours and lives on your device. Ending a session clears that session, and deleting the app permanently removes all data the app stored.

Children's privacy

NextFour is a general-audience organizer tool. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data works (for example, adding accounts that sync across devices), this policy will be updated on this page before that version is released, and the app's App Store privacy information will be updated to match.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the Support page.
