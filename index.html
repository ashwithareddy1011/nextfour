About NextFour

NextFour is the open-play organizer for pickleball sessions. Check players in by name and skill level, let the app build fair rotations automatically, keep score on every court, and put the Live Board up so the whole group can follow who's playing and who's next.

Everything runs on your device — no account is needed to start, and your sessions and player history are stored on the device only.

Frequently asked questions

How does the free plan work?

The Free plan covers 1 session at a time with up to 20 players, free forever. Just open the app and start — no account required.

What is the 14-day Pro trial?

When you create an account in the app, a 14-day Pro free trial starts automatically, with unlimited players during the trial. When the trial ends, the app simply continues on the Free plan.

Can I buy Pro?

Not yet. Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases — Premium is coming in a future update, and this page will be updated when it launches.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and only there. NextFour has no server and collects nothing — see our Privacy Policy for the full details.

I got a new phone. Does my player history move over?

Not in version 1.0 — because everything is stored on the device, history stays on the device where it was recorded.

How do I delete my data?

Ending a session clears that session. Deleting the app removes everything the app stored, completely and permanently.

A court looks stuck or the rotation looks wrong

Check that everyone who should be playing is checked in on the Players tab and nobody who left is still marked as waiting. The next game is always built from the players currently waiting.
